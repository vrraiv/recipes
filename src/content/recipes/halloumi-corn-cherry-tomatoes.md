Seared halloumi with sautéed corn, cherry tomatoes, jalapeño, and basil.

## Ingredients

- 60 ml extra-virgin olive oil (4 tbsp), plus more as needed
- 340–400 g halloumi (12–14 oz), cut into 2.5 cm (1-inch) cubes and patted dry
- 480 ml corn kernels (2 cups), fresh or frozen and thawed, from 2–3 ears
- 480 ml cherry tomatoes (2 cups), halved
- 1 jalapeño, thinly sliced, seeded or not
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 3 g Diamond Crystal kosher salt (1 tsp), plus more to taste
- 180 ml red onion (¾ cup), thinly sliced
- 120 ml fresh basil (½ cup), chopped, plus more for garnish
- 1 lime, cut into wedges
- Black pepper

## Method

1. **Sear the halloumi.** Heat 30 ml (2 tbsp) of the oil in a large skillet,
   ideally nonstick, over medium-high. Sear the cubes in batches in a single
   layer until golden, 1–2 minutes per side. Move to a paper-towel-lined plate.
2. **Cook the vegetables.** Add the remaining 30 ml (2 tbsp) oil to the pan,
   then the corn, tomatoes, jalapeño, cumin seeds, and salt. Sauté over
   medium-high until softened and juicy, 5–10 minutes.
3. **Toss off the heat.** Take the pan off the burner and fold in the
   halloumi, red onion, and basil.
4. **Season and serve.** Squeeze lime over the top, season with salt and
   plenty of black pepper, garnish with more basil, and serve right away.

## Notes

Canadian halloumi usually comes in 250 g packages. One and a half packages
covers the range given.

Salt is specified as Diamond Crystal kosher, which is light for its volume.
With table salt, go by the 3 g weight or halve the volume. Halloumi is already
salty, so taste before adding more at the end.

Pat the halloumi and thawed frozen corn dry before they go in the pan. Surface
water causes spattering and keeps the cheese from browning.

Without a nonstick pan, use a well-seasoned cast-iron skillet and add a little
more oil if the cheese starts to stick.

Halloumi turns rubbery as it cools, so this doesn't keep well. Serve it as soon
as it's tossed.
