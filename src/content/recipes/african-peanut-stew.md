A vegan stew of sweet potato, chickpeas, and greens in a peanut and tomato
broth.

## Ingredients

- 5 ml extra-virgin olive oil (1 tsp)
- 1 medium sweet onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 1 jalapeño, seeded and diced (optional)
- 1 medium sweet potato, peeled and cut into 1 cm (½-inch) pieces
- 1 can (796 ml / 28 oz) diced tomatoes, with their juices
- 80 ml natural peanut butter (⅓ cup)
- 960 ml vegetable broth (4 cups), plus more as needed
- 1½ tsp chili powder
- ¼ tsp cayenne pepper (optional)
- 1 can (425 g / 15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 2 handfuls baby spinach, or kale with stems removed, torn
- Fine sea salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- Fresh cilantro or parsley, for serving
- Roasted peanuts, for serving

## Method

1. **Soften the aromatics.** Heat the oil in a large saucepan over medium heat.
   Cook the onion and garlic about 5 minutes, until the onion is translucent.
2. **Add the vegetables** — bell pepper, jalapeño if using, sweet potato, and
   the tomatoes with their juices. Raise the heat to medium-high, simmer 5
   minutes, and season with salt and pepper.
3. **Loosen the peanut butter.** Whisk it with 240 ml (1 cup) of the broth in a
   bowl until smooth, then stir it into the pot with the remaining broth, the
   chili powder, and the cayenne if using.
4. **Simmer covered** over medium-low heat for 10–20 minutes, until the sweet
   potato is fork-tender.
5. **Finish.** Stir in the chickpeas and greens and cook until the greens wilt.
   Season to taste.
6. **Serve** garnished with cilantro or parsley and roasted peanuts.

## Notes

Canadian chickpea cans are usually 540 ml (19 oz), larger than the 15 oz can
specified. Use about three-quarters of one, or the whole can for a thicker stew.

Add more broth if the stew gets too thick as it simmers or when reheating.

Serve with cooked rice on the side, or stirred in, to make it more filling.

The jalapeño and cayenne are both optional. Leave them out for a mild stew.
