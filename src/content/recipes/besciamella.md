A garlic béchamel with melted mozzarella and nutmeg.

## Ingredients

- 28 g unsalted butter (2 tbsp)
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 2 garlic cloves, finely minced or grated on a microplane
- 480 ml whole milk (2 cups)
- 225 g low-moisture whole-milk mozzarella, grated (½ lb)
- ¼ tsp freshly grated nutmeg
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. **Melt the butter** in a medium saucepan over medium-high heat, stirring
   occasionally, until the foaming subsides, about 1 minute.
2. **Cook the roux.** Whisk in the flour and cook until light blond and
   slightly nutty-smelling, about 1 minute. Stir in the garlic.
3. **Add the milk** in a steady stream, whisking constantly, until fully
   incorporated. Bring to a simmer; it should thicken.
4. **Melt in the cheese.** Take the pan off the heat, add the mozzarella and
   nutmeg, and whisk until the cheese is fully melted.
5. **Simmer again and season.** Return to a simmer, whisking constantly, then
   take off the heat and season with salt and pepper. Set aside until needed.

## Notes

Use low-moisture mozzarella, the firm block kind, not fresh mozzarella. Fresh
mozzarella carries much more water and will thin the sauce.

Keep whisking once the milk goes in. The sauce catches on the bottom of the pan
quickly over medium-high heat.

The sauce thickens and forms a skin as it cools. Press plastic wrap directly on
the surface if it has to sit, and loosen it with a splash of milk over low heat
before using.
