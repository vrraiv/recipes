Light, crisp buttermilk waffles, leavened mostly by steam.

## Ingredients

- 185 g all-purpose flour (1⅓ cups)
- 45 g powdered sugar (¼ cup plus 2 tbsp)
- 2 g Diamond Crystal kosher salt (½ tsp)
- 3 g baking soda (½ tsp)
- 2 g baking powder (½ tsp)
- 1 vanilla bean, split and scraped
- 395 g cultured lowfat buttermilk (1¾ cups)
- 40 g unsalted butter, melted (3 tbsp)
- 70 g egg whites (2 large)
- 5 ml vanilla extract (1 tsp)
- Maple syrup, for serving

## Method

1. **Combine the dry ingredients** in a large bowl with the vanilla bean
   scrapings, working the seeds through by hand until evenly dispersed.
2. **Add the wet ingredients** — buttermilk, melted butter, egg whites, vanilla
   extract — and whisk smooth.
3. **Preheat the iron** to medium. Use roughly half the batter for an 8-inch
   square iron, about a third for a 7-inch Belgian.
4. **Cook about 5 minutes**, lid closed, until golden but still steaming.
   Timing shifts with the depth and heat of the machine.
5. **Serve immediately** with maple syrup, and cook the rest of the batter the
   same way.

## Notes

Weigh rather than measure if you can. Both the flour and the buttermilk fall on
awkward cup fractions because the recipe was written to weights, and the
buttermilk-to-flour ratio is doing the structural work — a loosely packed extra
quarter-cup of flour flattens it.

Salt is specified as Diamond Crystal kosher, which is unusually light for its
volume. With table salt, go by the 2 g weight or halve the volume.

Powdered sugar rather than granulated is deliberate — the cornstarch in it
contributes to the crisp exterior.

Egg whites only, no yolks. Yolks would add fat and softness, which is the
opposite of what this recipe is after.

Waffles soften as they sit. If you're cooking for more than two, hold the
finished ones directly on the oven rack at 200°F rather than stacking them on a
plate, where trapped steam undoes the crust.
