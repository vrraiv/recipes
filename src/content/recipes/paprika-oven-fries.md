Crisp oven fries of red-skinned potatoes with garlic, smoked paprika, and
semolina.

## Ingredients

- 1.2 kg large red-skinned potatoes, about 6, skin on, cut into 1.5 cm (½-inch) batons (2 lb 10 oz)
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp paprika
- 60 ml sunflower oil (¼ cup)
- 2 tsp fine semolina
- 1½ tsp coarse sea salt, plus more for the cooking water
- Black pepper, plenty

## Method

1. **Heat the oven** to 240°C (465°F), or 220°C (425°F) convection. Line two
   large baking sheets with parchment.
2. **Blanch the potatoes.** Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil
   over high heat, add the batons, and cook 5 minutes. Drain.
3. **Toss with the seasonings.** In a bowl, combine the potatoes with the
   garlic, both paprikas, oil, semolina, the 1½ tsp salt, and plenty of pepper.
   Mix thoroughly but gently so the potatoes don't break.
4. **Spread out** on the two sheets, well spaced so they brown rather than
   steam.
5. **Roast about 35 minutes**, turning once halfway, until cooked through, dark
   golden, and crisp.

## Notes

Keep the batons spaced out on two sheets. Crowded on one, they steam and stay
pale.

Blanched potatoes are fragile. Toss with a spatula or your hands rather than
stirring hard.

Any neutral oil works in place of sunflower. Without semolina, a little fine
cornmeal does a similar job on the crust.
