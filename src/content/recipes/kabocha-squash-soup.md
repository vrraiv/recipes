A silky puréed soup of kabocha squash, onion, and cream.

## Ingredients

- ½ onion, thinly sliced
- 635 g kabocha squash (1.4 lb), seeded and cut into chunks
- 14 g unsalted butter (1 tbsp)
- 480 ml vegetable broth (2 cups)
- 360 ml whole milk (1½ cups)
- 120 ml heavy (whipping) cream (½ cup)
- 6 g Diamond Crystal kosher salt (2 tsp)
- ⅛ tsp freshly ground black pepper
- Parsley, chopped, for garnish

## Method

1. **Cook the onion.** In a pot, heat the butter over medium heat and cook the
   onion until soft and browned.
2. **Add the kabocha** and sauté to coat it with the butter.
3. **Simmer.** Add the vegetable broth and bring to a boil, then reduce the heat
   and simmer 12–15 minutes, until a skewer slides easily into the kabocha.
4. **Purée** the soup until smooth, in batches in a blender or with an immersion
   blender.
5. **Add the dairy.** Stir in the milk and cream until combined. Do not let the
   soup boil.
6. **Season** with the salt and pepper and stir over moderately low heat until
   hot. Serve garnished with chopped parsley.

## Notes

Vegetable broth keeps this vegetarian; chicken stock works the same way if that
isn't a concern.

Salt is specified as Diamond Crystal kosher, which is light for its volume. With
table salt, go by the 6 g weight or halve the volume.

Don't let the soup boil once the milk and cream are in, or the dairy can break.
