Whole wheat apple muffins, sweetened with banana and maple syrup.

## Ingredients

- `SOMETHING_TK` whole wheat flour
- `SOMETHING_TK` all-purpose flour
- `SOMETHING_TK` baking soda
- `SOMETHING_TK` salt
- `SOMETHING_TK` ground cinnamon
- `SOMETHING_TK` unsalted butter, melted
- 60 g ripe banana, mashed (in place of the original's brown sugar)
- `SOMETHING_TK` granulated sugar (three-quarters of the original weight)
- `SOMETHING_TK` maple syrup (one-quarter of the original granulated sugar
  weight)
- `SOMETHING_TK` eggs
- `SOMETHING_TK` dairy (milk, buttermilk, or yogurt — per the original)
- `SOMETHING_TK` apples, cored and chopped, unpeeled

## Method

1. **Heat the oven** to `SOMETHING_TK` and grease or line a muffin tin.
2. **Whisk the dry ingredients** — both flours, baking soda, salt, and
   cinnamon — in a large bowl.
3. **Mix the wet ingredients** in a second bowl: melted butter, mashed banana,
   granulated sugar, maple syrup, eggs, and the dairy.
4. **Combine.** Stir the wet into the dry until just mixed, then fold in the
   apples.
5. **Fill the cups** and bake `SOMETHING_TK` minutes, until a tester comes out
   clean.

## Notes

The banana and maple syrup add moisture the original's dry sugars don't. The
maple syrup is weighed as a straight quarter of the granulated sugar, not
converted for sweetness.

Leaving the apple peel on saves a step and adds colour; chop the pieces small
so the peel doesn't read as tough.
